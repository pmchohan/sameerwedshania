# Surface brief: the invitation

Mode: Experience. Visitor: a wedding guest on a phone, opened from WhatsApp. Job: see who is marrying, which events they are invited to, when and where, then open the map. Binding constraints live in PRODUCT.md and docs/design-brief.md (no rust, beige or cream; quiet language toggle).

Memorable moment: the tap on the keystone that locks the last tiles and opens the arch onto the names.

Unresolved: none for the first surface. Tile motifs, type pairing, per-event panel treatment and desktop treatment are recorded in `DESIGN.md`; remaining placeholders are product facts in config.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The invitation is one tiled mihrab wall that assembles itself, then continues as a sequence of tile panels, one per event. It refuses the gold-script-over-mandala card template and the doors-or-envelope opening.

OWN-WORLD: Wazir Khan kashi-kari. Deep cobalt ground, turquoise, leaf green, cool white and bright yellow-gold outlines. Square module tiles in two or three colours per panel, pointed arch frames, cartouches that hold the text. Inline SVG and CSS only, flat glaze, no soft shadows.

STORY: The guest taps, watches tiles lock into an arch, reads their name and who is marrying, scrolls through only their own events, opens the map. They believe it was made for them and their family.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait 390x844. Cobalt ground with loose scattered tiles. A pointed arch outlined in gold fills the middle of the screen. A keystone tile at the apex is the tap target, with the tap-to-open label beneath. The language toggle sits small at the top right. After the tap the tiles lock in and the cartouche inside the arch carries the couple's names.

FORM: Wazir Khan kashi-kari tile panels, position 3 of 7, seed:6037583288640bcd. Two raises: one invisible square module governs every tile, text block and the arch (from Studio Dumbar); the ground shifts like a lighting cue down the scroll, cobalt to turquoise to white (from Cyclorama).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
